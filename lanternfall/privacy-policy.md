# Lanternfall — Privacy Policy
*Last updated: November 26, 2026*

# ENGLISH (Українська версія — нижче)

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Lanternfall (the "App", package com.svtbn.lanternfall), developed by Sviatoslav Binovskyi (the "Developer", "we"), handles information. Contact: santonase@gmail.com

## 1. Summary
- You can play without creating an account. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or precise location.
- The Developer does not run its own servers and does not sell personal data.
- The App shows ads through Google AdMob, which collects some data from your device as described below and in Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 2. Data stored on your device
Game progress (stars, embers, Altar blessings) and the tutorial flag are stored locally on your device. This data stays on your device and is removed when you uninstall the App.

## 3. Advertising — Google AdMob
The App shows optional rewarded video ads and occasional full-screen ads between levels (never during a battle) through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process:
- device identifiers, such as the Android Advertising ID;
- approximate location derived from your IP address;
- app usage, diagnostics and performance data (for example crash logs and ad interactions).
This data is used to show, measure and personalize ads, to prevent fraud, and for analytics. It is shared with Google and, depending on your choices, with Google's certified advertising partners.
How Google uses data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
You can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on the device).

## 4. Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland
If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the App asks for your consent through Google's consent tool (a certified consent management platform) before personalized ads are shown. You can change or withdraw your consent at any time in the App: Codex → Ad privacy settings.

## 5. Children
The App is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us at santonase@gmail.com.

## 6. Data retention and deletion
The Developer does not keep your personal data on its own servers. To delete data:
- local game data — uninstall the App or clear its data in Android settings;
- advertising data — reset or delete your Advertising ID in device settings; data held by Google is subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
For any privacy question or request, write to santonase@gmail.com.

## 7. Your rights
Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your data, to object to processing, and to withdraw consent. To exercise these rights, use the in-app Ad privacy settings or contact santonase@gmail.com. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

## 8. Security
Data sent by Google AdMob is encrypted in transit.

## 9. Changes
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be published at this address with a new "Last updated" date.

## 10. Contact
Sviatoslav Binovskyi — santonase@gmail.com

- приблизне місцезнаходження, визначене за IP-адресою;

Ці дані використовуються, щоб показувати, вимірювати й персоналізувати рекламу, запобігати шахрайству та для аналітики. Вони передаються Google і, залежно від вашого вибору, сертифікованим рекламним партнерам Google.
Як Google використовує дані: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
Ви можете скинути або видалити рекламний ідентифікатор чи відмовитися від персоналізованої реклами в налаштуваннях пристрою (Налаштування → Google → Реклама або Налаштування → Конфіденційність → Реклама, залежно від пристрою).
